Pokémon Club is a fun and welcoming program for children aged 6-11 years old that helps develop important life skills through playing the Pokémon Trading Card Game (TCG).

It provides an inclusive space for young players to think strategically, build social-emotional skills, make new friends, and connect with fellow Pokémon fans.

Mana Rock Gaming would also like to extend a special thank you to our program partner, the Kershaw County Library System, for helping us bring the joy of gaming to everyone in our community!
