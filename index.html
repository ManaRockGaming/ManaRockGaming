Who Are We?
The Mana
Rock Gaming
Foundation

At Mana Rock Gaming, we believe games are more than just a way to have fun, they’re a way to learn, create, explore, and bring people together.

Through classes, workshops, gaming events, conventions, and community programs, we bring education and play together. Our programs encourage creativity, teamwork, problem-solving, computer science, game development, history, and much more.

Whether you're rolling dice, painting an army, writing your first line of code, designing a game, or exploring history through a tabletop battlefield, there's always something new to discover.

Mana Rock Gaming is building a community where everyone has a place to play, create, and learn together.

How Can We Help You?

OUR PROGRAMS

💻
Programming Classes

Learn to code, build games, and bring your ideas to life. Our programming classes give students hands-on experience with coding, game development, and computer science.

⚔️
Community Events

Bringing people together through games, creativity, and shared experiences. Our community events create welcoming spaces for people to connect, learn, and have fun.

🎲
Board Game Night

Roll the dice, draw a card, and find your next favorite game! Our traveling Board Game Nights are a chance to discover new games, meet new people, and enjoy some friendly competition.
