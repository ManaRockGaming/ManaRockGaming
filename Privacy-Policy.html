Section 1. Who We Are

The purpose of this policy is to establish clear privacy controls and procedures.

Section 2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information through forms, surveys, subscriptions, donations, or other interactions:

Personal Information: Such as name, email address, phone number, mailing address, age, occupation, graduation status, graduation year, or affiliation.

Business Information: Such as organization name, business contact info, position/title, or public records data.

Technical Information: Such as IP address, browser type, device information, and browsing activity using cookies or analytics tools.

Section 3. How We Use Your Information

We may use the information collected for purposes including:

Communication and outreach.

Event organization and participation.

Volunteer or donor engagement.

Marketing and promotional campaigns.

Section 4. Information Sharing

We may share your information with trusted third parties, including:

Partner organizations and sponsors.

Political or advocacy groups with aligned interests.

Service providers (e.g., email platforms, analytics tools)

We do not sell your information for profit. However, we reserve the right to share personal and business information for lawful political, educational, or organizational purposes in alignment with our mission.

Section 5. Your Choices

You may opt out of communications or request your data be updated or removed at any time by contacting us at ManaRockGaming@gmail.com

Section 6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information but cannot guarantee complete security. Use of the website is at your own risk.

Section 7. Links To Other Sites

Our website may contain links to third-party sites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their privacy policies independently.

Section 8. Changes To This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy at any time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Section 9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at ManaRockGaming@gmail.com
