Who Are We?
The Mana
Rock Gaming
Foundation

The mission of Mana Rock Gaming is to promote public education, creativity, and community engagement in South Carolina through interactive entertainment mediums, including but not limited to board games, video games, roleplaying games, miniature wargames, and related digital and tabletop systems. We seek to advance game design, coding, system development, and historical simulation as educational, artistic, and collaborative disciplines.

Mana Rock Gaming conducts and supports educational programming, workshops, conventions, and community events that promote learning in computer science, game development, history-based education, and related fields.

Mana Rock Gaming further promotes the accessibility and growth of collaborative recreational and educational play, and seeks to ensure that individuals across South Carolina have opportunities to participate in shared interactive experiences that foster learning, creativity, and community.

Donate To Support Our Mission

By supporting the Mana Rock Gaming Foundation you are helping us to build community through the love of gaming. Every donation helps fund programming classes, community events, board game nights, and opportunities for people to play, create, learn, and connect.
